## 🔷 ARCHITECTURE PATTERNS USED

### Enterprise Patterns ✅
- **Domain-Driven Design** (bounded contexts per role)
- **Repository Pattern** (ready for EF implementation)
- **Service Locator** (dependency injection)
- **Event Sourcing** (event-driven architecture)
- **CQRS Ready** (separation of concerns)
- **Audit Trail** (compliance and forensics)

### SOLID Principles ✅
- **Single Responsibility**: Each service does one job
- **Open/Closed**: Extensible without modification
- **Liskov Substitution**: Interfaces are interchangeable
- **Interface Segregation**: Role-specific contracts
- **Dependency Inversion**: Abstractions, not implementations

### Scalability Patterns ✅
- **Horizontal Scaling**: Stateless services
- **Event-Driven**: Decoupled components
- **Async/Await**: Non-blocking operations
- **Caching Ready**: Redis integration points
- **Database Queries**: Indexed, filtered
